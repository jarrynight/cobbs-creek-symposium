The Cobbs Creek Symposium is a day of celebration, connection, and collective learning dedicated to the health and revitalization of Cobbs Creek. Bringing together community organizers, volunteers, educators, researchers, advocates, and all those who care about the creek’s future, the symposium will create space to share knowledge, exchange ideas, celebrate ongoing efforts, and strengthen relationships. Through meaningful conversations and shared experiences, we will explore how collaboration, partnerships, and a collective vision can advance our shared goals for a healthier, more vibrant, and resilient Cobbs Creek. This is an opportunity to recognize the power of community, build momentum, and imagine what we can accomplish together.

The symposium will feature creative workshops, key conversations and opportunities to learn from the generations of people working on important environmental stewardship and education in Cobbs Creek, including:

  • Youth research and environmental stewardship
  • Local Leadership & Current Projects
  • Water Quality & Environmental Restoration
  • Recreation & Park Greening
  • Art & Sustainability
  • Policy Integration & Alignment
